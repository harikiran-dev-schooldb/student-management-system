# SchoolDB Product Overview

SchoolDB is a multi-tenant School ERP SaaS platform designed to digitize academic and administrative operations for educational institutions.

The platform enables schools to manage:

• Students
• Teachers
• Classes
• Attendance
• Exams
• Fees
• Reports

## Architecture

SchoolDB follows a modern SaaS architecture:

Tenant Routing:
schooldb.co.in/[schoolId]

API Structure:
/api/v1/tenants/[schoolId]/*

## Target Users

• Private Schools
• Coaching Institutes
• Training Academies
• School Chains
